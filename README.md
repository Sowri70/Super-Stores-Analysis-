Super Stores Sales Analysis 

Dataset :   Fortunately, I happen to receive a clean dataset.  I did not have to do any cleaning.  

Brief Analysis :

Sales tend to peak at the end of the year, while business activity typically slows down during April, May, and June. Performance begins to pick up again from July onward.

California leads in overall sales, followed by New York, Texas, and Washington. The Western region generates the highest sales, contributing $0.74 million, followed by the Eastern, Central, and Southern regions.

Technology products are the most in demand, closely followed by Furniture and Office Supplies.

There is considerable scope to improve performance in the Corporate customer segment, and management should explore opportunities for growth in this area.
